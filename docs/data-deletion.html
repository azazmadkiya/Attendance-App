Google Play Data Safety

Account & Data Deletion Policy

Application: Attendance App (Haazri Pro)
Developer: Azaz Madkiya

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, this page describes how users can delete their accounts and all associated workforce data from Attendance App (Haazri Pro).

Option 1: In-App Immediate Local Data Wipe

  1. Open Attendance App on your Android phone.
  2. Tap on the Settings tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to Legal & App Policies and select Data Safety & Permissions.
  4. Tap Wipe All Local App Data and confirm the prompt.
  5. All local database files, workers, attendance records, and preferences will be permanently wiped immediately.

Option 2: Cloud Account & Data Deletion Request (Online)

If you logged into the application using Google Sign-In / Firebase and wish to delete your cloud profile and synchronized backups from our backend:

Send an email to our developer support team with your registered email address:

Recipient: azazmadkiya@gmail.com
Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - Attendance App
Content: Include the mobile number or Google email associated with your account.

Request Deletion via Email

What Data Is Deleted?

  • User profile name, email, phone number, firm name.
  • All employee records, daily attendance entries, timestamps, and GPS geofence logs.
  • All cashbook entries, advance salary payments, and financial summaries.
  • Firebase authentication tokens and cloud database documents.

Retention Period: Data is removed immediately from your local device upon local reset, and cloud records are purged within 48 hours of a verified email request.
